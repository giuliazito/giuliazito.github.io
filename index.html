Case Study · Data Cleaning & Analysis

The goal and motive of this project were to practice my data cleaning and visualization skills using Python's pandas, matplotlib and seaborn libraries.

The data is from Kaggle, and it represents New York City Airbnb open data.

The process I followed

  1. Column standardization
  2. Duplicate dropping
  3. Data type formatting
  4. Missing values
  5. Value mapping
  6. Outlier handling

Findings

For the analysis and visualization, my goal was to determine price and location for a potential new listing.

I started out looking at how the different room types were priced across the boroughs of NYC, and then focused on Brooklyn's demand and supply distribution. Using number of listings and median monthly reviews as a proxy for supply and demand, I identified the top three neighborhood areas of Brooklyn that have a low number of listings per monthly review rate.

This analysis indicates the best opportunity areas for a potential new listing and provides the median price per room type in those areas.

In Progress · Machine Learning & Financial Analysis

This project is a work in progress. The goal is to build a machine learning model that predicts which customers are likely to churn, then translate those predictions into an estimate of the financial risk they represent.

Planned approach

What this will show

Once the model is trained, the plan is to quantify the revenue at risk from predicted churners and estimate the potential financial impact of retention efforts, so the results speak to both model accuracy and business value.
