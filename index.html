A work-in-progress analysis of NYC Airbnb open data: cleaning it thoroughly first, so the factors behind listing prices can be trusted.

The raw file has 102,599 listings across 26 columns. I standardized column names, removed 541 duplicate rows, converted price and service fee from text to numbers, parsed review dates, and handled missing values by filling in what could be inferred, labeling missing categories as Unknown, and dropping rows rather than fabricating data.

  • Fixed a misspelled borough ("brookln" to Brooklyn) and standardized text formatting.
  • Corrected 414 negative availability values and capped 2,664 impossible ones (over 365 days) at 365.
  • Corrected 13 negative minimum-night values and capped 31 that were over 365.
  • Removed a review date that was set in the future.

The graph above compares availability before and after cleaning. Next up: exploring how borough, room type, reviews and host factors relate to price, with early charts already covering price by room type, a correlation heatmap and a map of listings.
