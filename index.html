Projects

The goal and motive of this project were to practice my data cleaning and visualization skills using Python's pandas, matplotlib and seaborn libraries.

The data is from Kaggle, and it represents New York City Airbnb open data.

The process I followed for this project is the following:

  • Column standardization
  • Duplicate dropping
  • Data type formatting
  • Missing values
  • Value mapping
  • Outlier handling

For the analysis and visualization, my goal was to determine price and location for a potential new listing. I started out looking at how the different room types were priced across the boroughs of NYC, and then focused on Brooklyn's demand and supply distribution. Using number of listings and median reviews per month as a proxy for demand and supply, I identified the top three areas of Brooklyn with a lower number of listings per monthly review, indicating the best opportunity areas for a potential new listing and the median price per room type in that area.
